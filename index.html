Altera Apps

Small, quiet apps for iPhone.

Altera Apps is a one-person studio. The work is aimed at software that is calm to use and honest about what it does: no accounts to make, nothing tracked, nothing sold, and no subscription for things that should never have been subscriptions.

Apps

Sit Still

A quiet room to focus in.

A focus timer built around nineteen illustrated rooms, each with its own recorded ambience and a cat asleep somewhere in it. Blocks what you ask it to block, keeps a record of what you actually sat through, and keeps all of it on your phone.
