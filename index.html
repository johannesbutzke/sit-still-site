A focus timer built around rooms

Set a length, pick a room, and stay in it. The room fills the screen — a tea house in Kyoto, an attic in Paris, a library in Vienna — with its own sound playing underneath and a cat asleep somewhere in it. When the time is up you get a few whiskers and a line to write down what you actually did.

Nineteen illustrated rooms

Seventeen permanent ones, free and open from the first launch, plus two seasonal rooms that change every month. Each has its own recorded ambience, levelled so moving between rooms is never a lurch.

A focus shield, if you want one

Three modes: Companionship blocks nothing, Whitelist lets a chosen set of apps through, Strict blocks everything for the length of the session. Built on Apple's own Screen Time frameworks.

A bedtime lock

Choose a window and the same shield comes up on a schedule, so the phone stops being interesting at the hour you decided rather than the hour you give in.

Tasks, and a record

A task list that syncs both ways with Apple Reminders if you want it to, and a plain chart of what you have actually sat through.

Cats

Six companions, all free, picked on taste rather than earned. Focusing pays whiskers, and whiskers buy the seasonal rooms and things for the cat to wear. Nothing else costs anything.

Sit Still is for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. There is no account, no sign-in, and it works with no connection at all.

Support

Something wrong, or an idea? Email sitstillapp@gmail.com. Settings → About → Send feedback opens the same address with your version, build, iOS version and device model already filled in, which makes anything reproducible much faster to fix. Expect a reply within a few days.

The room stops playing when I lock the screen

Sound has to be on for a session to keep running in the background. With room sound switched off, iOS suspends the app, and a countdown will not finish or chime until you open it again. Settings → The room has the switch.

The room sound stops my music

It shouldn't — room sound is mixed under whatever else is playing rather than interrupting it, and it ignores the ring/silent switch. If it is louder than you want it under music, Settings → The room has a volume slider and a Listen button.

Blocked apps aren't being blocked

Check Settings → Focus shield: Companionship is the default and blocks nothing. Whitelist and Strict both need Screen Time access, which iOS asks for the first time you pick one. Screen Time cannot shield some system apps, and it quietly stops working past 50 chosen apps or websites.

Apps are still blocked and no session is running

Open Sit Still. A shield is a system setting rather than something the app holds open, so it survives a force-quit — and opening the app clears any shield with no session behind it. If you have deleted the app while a shield was up, reinstall it, open it once, and the shield comes down.

The bedtime lock never fires

iOS refuses to monitor a window shorter than 15 minutes, and the start and end callbacks only arrive while the device is in use — a phone face-down at 22:30 gets its lock when it is next picked up.

My tasks and Reminders disagree

The reminder wins on any conflict. Scope is Sit Still's own Reminders list plus reminders you deliberately linked; a reminder added to another list is not picked up. Deleting travels both ways — deleting a task deletes its reminder, and vice versa.

I left a session by accident and lost the time

Leaving takes a deliberate 1.3-second hold precisely so it doesn't happen by accident, and time left that way is not banked. Finishing early is different: an open-ended session, or a countdown paused past the first minute, banks what you did sit through.

How do I earn whiskers, and what are they for?

Two per minute of a completed session, with a bonus past half an hour, and roughly a one-in-four chance that an accessory you don't own turns up instead. They buy the two seasonal rooms of the month and things for the cat to wear. Every permanent room and every cat is free.

A seasonal room disappeared

Seasonal rooms rotate at the end of the month. One bought with whiskers stays yours; the ones you didn't buy are replaced by the next two.

Saving a room as wallpaper does nothing

Settings → The room → Save room as wallpaper needs permission to add to Photos, which iOS asks for once. If it was declined, turn it back on in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. The app can add images and cannot read your library.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything Sit Still knows lives on the device and goes with it — there is no account and no copy anywhere else.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026.

Sit Still collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in and no server. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What stays on your device

  • Your sessions, tasks, notes, whiskers balance, cats and accessories.
  • Your settings, including the focus shield mode and bedtime schedule.
  • The apps you chose to shield. These come back from Apple's own picker as opaque tokens the app cannot read or resolve into names, and are stored in a shared container on the device only.

All of it lives in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Screen Time

Sit Still uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity) to shield apps during a session and during a bedtime window you set. It reads no Screen Time usage data — not which apps you open, not for how long, not how often. It only raises and lowers shields you asked for, on the device they were asked for.

Reminders

With Reminders sync switched on, the app reads and writes its own Reminders list, plus any individual reminder you deliberately linked. That happens through Apple's EventKit on your device. Nothing is copied off it.

Photos

Save room as wallpaper writes one image to your photo library when you tap it. The permission is add-only: the app cannot read your library.

Feedback email

Sending feedback opens your own mail app with a message you can read and edit before sending. If you send it, that email reaches sitstillapp@gmail.com and nowhere else. Not sending it means nothing is transmitted at all.

Analytics, ads and third parties

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no third-party code of any kind. Nothing is sold, shared or profiled, because nothing is collected.

Apple may collect its own aggregate, anonymised statistics about App Store downloads and crashes under its own privacy policy. Those never reach the developer as anything identifying you.

Children

Sit Still is rated 4+ and safe for any age, but it is not directed at children and knowingly collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
