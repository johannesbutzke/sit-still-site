Common questions

Does it work on iPad and Android tablets?

Yes. It turns with the device, and every room becomes a wide painting. On Android tablets the paintings download from Google Play the first time; until then you see the phone versions. Sit Still isn’t available on Mac.

The sound stops when I lock the screen

It shouldn’t. Something interrupted it: a call, an alarm or Siri, plugging into a car, or, on Android, unplugging headphones. Open the app to bring it back. The session’s clock keeps running either way.

My music stops when a session starts

The room’s sound plays under your music, so the cause is usually the blocking. Strict blocks everything, and Whitelist blocks every app you haven’t added, music apps included. Add your music app in Settings → Focus shield → Whitelist, or use Companionship, which blocks nothing.

Blocked apps aren’t being blocked

Check Settings → Focus shield: Companionship, the default, blocks nothing. Whitelist and Strict need permission, and their rows say when it’s missing.

iPhone: Screen Time access. Some system apps can’t be blocked.
Android: Usage access and Display over other apps, both in Android’s Settings. Your home screen and Settings are never blocked.

Apps are still blocked with no session running

A blocking rule or the bedtime lock is probably running; Settings → Focus shield shows which, and until when. If none is, open Sit Still: on iPhone that clears any leftover block. If you deleted the app while something was blocked, reinstall it and open it once.

How do I block apps outside a session?

Settings → Focus shield → Blocking on a schedule. A rule blocks the apps you choose:

  • Schedule: between two times, on the days you pick.
  • Daily limit: after so many minutes a day.
  • In one go: after so many minutes without a break.

You can have up to eight rules. They run whether or not you open the app.

A rule or the bedtime lock never starts

Check that it has apps chosen.

iPhone: iOS needs a window of at least 15 minutes, and may wait until you next pick up the phone.
Android: allow Alarms & reminders in Android’s Settings for exact timing.

I can’t switch off a rule or the bedtime lock

It’s in hard mode and running, so it can’t be changed until its hours end. It has a padlock beside its name. For a real emergency, use the emergency pass.

Why does switching a rule off take twenty seconds?

A running rule asks for a moment with the cat first: tap Begin, breathe for twenty seconds, then decide. Outside its hours, a rule switches off with a tap.

What is the emergency pass?

Once a week, it lifts every rule and the bedtime lock for an hour, hard mode included. It works only while something in hard mode is blocking. The first pass is free; after that each costs 200 whiskers. Find it in Settings → Focus shield.

I can’t tell which apps I picked

iPhone: tap Expand under the icons to see their names. iOS shows them; Sit Still itself never learns which apps you picked.
Android: the list shows names from the start.

Do tasks sync with Reminders?

iPhone: yes, if you switch it on. It syncs Sit Still’s own list and any reminder you link; on a conflict, the reminder wins.
Android: no. Instead, an optional morning notification lists what’s due.

I left a session by accident

Leaving takes a deliberate hold, and that time isn’t counted. To keep what you’ve done, finish an open-ended session, or pause a countdown after its first minute.

Can I change the pomodoro’s lengths?

Yes: Settings → Timer → Pomodoro. New lengths start with the next session.

Can I start a session without opening the app?

iPhone: ask Siri to “Start a session in Sit Still”, or use the Shortcuts app.
Android: touch and hold the app icon.

Where are the widgets?

They come with Premium. Settings → Widgets shows how to add them.

How do whiskers work?

Two per minute of a finished session, ten extra for half an hour or more, up to 250 a day. They buy seasonal rooms, things for your cat to wear, and emergency passes. Money never buys whiskers.

What does Premium include?

Every room and cat, the pomodoro, blocking rules, the bedtime lock, hard mode, widgets and cat accessories. Restore purchases and Manage subscription are in Settings → Premium.

A seasonal room disappeared

Seasonal rooms change at the end of each month. One you bought is yours for good; the rest are gone. The date is checked online, so changing your phone’s clock won’t bring them back.

Where does Save as wallpaper put the picture?

The button is on the Rooms tab. A phone gets a picture its own shape. An iPad chooses between iPad and Mac shapes; an Android tablet gets its own sideways shape.

iPhone: in Photos. If nothing appears, allow adding photos in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.
Android: in your gallery, under Pictures/Sit Still.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

iPhone: turn on Settings → Backup → iCloud backup, then restore on the new phone. It’s off until you turn it on.
Android: Android’s own backup to Google can include it, if you have that switched on.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. There’s no account and no copy anywhere else, except a backup you turned on yourself. To remove the iCloud one, use Settings → Backup → Delete backup from iCloud first.
